# CIPHEUR AAMAS 2027 manuscript draft

`CIPHEUR_AAMAS2027.tex` uses the supplied AAMAS 2027 anonymous Research Paper Track class and copyright block. `CIPHEUR_references.bib` provides the cited reference entries. The four PDF plots and trajectory/timing LaTeX fragment derive from the audited 900-second log analysis. Additional fragments report completed baseline, framework-control, offline-generation, and cross-source results.

The registered C05 comparison has eight constraint views of the single physical source `CP-SCALE-AU-L002`, five seeds per view, and four arms with 40 valid results each. The independent audit reports 160/160 valid arm--graph--seed results and is stored at `研究二-正式方法探索版本/v0.6.0/experiments/c05_900s_codex_20261009/local_mirror_900s/analysis/c05_900s_independent_audit.json` (SHA-256 `cd44c6fac6737175a8e557b8606ff418950df8a8bb867b0a7d79e99255c000dc`). Its `positions.csv` has SHA-256 `741b806379cb6493c50b499bb849489e354e4e9f5b164a6bc7de9ca7cc286138`. The eight-view equal-weight means in contact-seconds are 1,138,760.36872255 (CIPHEUR-online), 1,138,451.911341275 (CIPHEUR-off-EXP), 1,138,417.6763745 (CIPHEUR-off-VAL), and 1,134,942.785685025 (CHILS-p4-custom). The combined main table also includes separately audited completed baselines and CIPHEUR-off-GEN; its CSV and source hashes are recorded in `combined_table_manifest.json`. CIPHEUR-off-RAND is included by the table builder only after its separate eight-view, five-seed audit is complete.

The current KNN/LinUCB selectors are source-defined, within-run updating controls. The display names `CIPHEUR-off-EXP` and `CIPHEUR-off-VAL` describe their selection mechanisms and lack of runtime model calls; the available records do not attribute them to LLM synthesis. `CIPHEUR-off-RAND` is a random programmatic selector with no deployment LLM calls and no LLM-generation claim. The L003 source group has an author-provided non-use attestation, and its completed arms are reported separately from the L002 frame.
